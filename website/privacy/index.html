Who we are

Contact: contact@singitai.app.

What we process

  • Your wallet address. You sign in with an EVM or Solana wallet; the address is your account. We keep the sign-in message we issued and check your signature against it. Session tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • API keys. Keys are shown once and stored only as hashes, with each key's limits and what it has spent.
  • Spending limits and agent keys. When you give an agent a budget, SingIt creates an agent key for your account and keeps it encrypted on our server. On Base, the limits live in a contract on the public chain.
  • Payments and purchases. Payment and transaction records, balances, holds, and orders you place (for example gift cards, eSIMs and top-ups through Bitrefill). A gift card code is shown to you once and is not stored by us.
  • Chats in the web app. Your chats (titles, your messages and the answers) are stored on our server so you can come back to them. Deleting a chat deletes its messages.
  • Requests to the API and MCP server. To answer, we pass your request to the model or data provider that serves it. We do not write prompt text or model output to our logs. We record usage: model, token counts, cost and time.
  • Details you give the assistant. For example an email address you ask it to send things to, or a phone number for a call you request. For calls we keep the order and the last digits of the number; calls are not recorded.
  • Notifications. If you turn on browser notifications, the push subscription your browser gives us.
  • Technical data. IP addresses and timestamps for rate limits, free-trial limits and abuse prevention, plus error and availability records.
  • Earlier messaging channels. Earlier versions of SingIt worked through Telegram, WhatsApp and iMessage and processed identifiers and messages from those platforms. These channels are being discontinued; records from them are kept only until they are deleted.

Analytics

We measure product use with Umami, self-hosted on our own server and cookieless. Accounts are counted only by a salted one-way hash. We do not send wallet addresses, email addresses, transaction hashes or chat content to analytics, and we do not use analytics for advertising.

Why we process it

To run the features you ask for (answers, searches, purchases, payments within your limits), to keep your account and limits safe, to bill correctly, to prevent abuse, to fix errors, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Model and data providers that produce an answer you asked for: models are reached mainly through the Surplus Intelligence marketplace, private chat through Venice AI, and web search and page reading through Exa.
  • Merchants and services that complete a purchase or call you asked for, such as Bitrefill and Bland AI.
  • Payment and blockchain infrastructure, such as the Coinbase Developer Platform x402 facilitator and RPC providers for Base and Solana.
  • Hosting: Cloudflare for the website and our server hosting provider for the API.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect users and the service.

Public by design

Blockchain transactions are public and permanent: wallet addresses, amounts, times and transaction identifiers can be seen by anyone, and we cannot change or delete them. SingIt also offers a public spending report for any wallet address (categories and totals, no chat content) at app.singitai.app/v1/wallet/<address>/reconciliation.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to provide the service, keep accounting and security records, and meet legal obligations. Chats stay until you delete them. Hashed tokens and keys stay until they expire or are revoked.

Security

Access controls, encrypted transport, hashed tokens and keys, encrypted agent keys, and on-chain limits that hold even if our server is compromised. No system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

You can delete chats and revoke API keys and allowances in the app, or revoke a token approval on chain without us. To access, correct or delete your data, email contact@singitai.app from an address you can verify, with the wallet address of your account. We may ask you to sign a message with that wallet to prove it is yours. We keep what the law or fraud prevention requires, and cannot delete what is on a public blockchain.

International processing

Our providers may process data in other countries than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Children

SingIt is not for anyone under 18.

Changes

We update this policy when the service or the law changes, and change the effective date above when we do.
