Privacy Policy

What SingIt collects, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do. It covers the website, the web app, the API, the MCP server and the Telegram bot.

Effective November 2, 2026

Who we are

SingIt (“SingIt”, “we”, “us”) operates this service from Prague, Czech Republic, and is the controller of the personal data described here. SingIt is not operated by a registered company. Contact for anything about your data: contact@singitai.app.

This policy covers the website singitai.app, the web app and its API at app.singitai.app, the pay-per-request API at ask.singitai.app, the MCP server at app.singitai.app/mcp and the SingIt Telegram bot. It goes with our Terms of Service, which are written under Czech law; SingIt is for people aged 18 and over.

In short

  • There is no username or password. Your account is your wallet address. We never see or ask for your seed phrase or private key.
  • Questions you send through the API (with an API key or paid per request over x402) are passed to the model provider and are not written to our database or logs. Conversations in the web app chat are different: we store them so you can come back to them, until you delete them.
  • We use a cookieless analytics tool that runs on our own server. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Payments settle on public blockchains. Anyone can see wallet addresses and amounts there, and we cannot delete or change them.

What we collect

What we hold depends on which parts of SingIt you use.

Account and sign-in (web app)

  • Your wallet address (Base or Solana). You sign in by signing a message we issue; we keep that message briefly and check your signature against it. If you sign in with email, Google, Apple, X or Discord, the wallet is created and run by Reown (the company behind WalletConnect) and we receive only the wallet address and the signature, not your email or social profile.
  • A session. The sign-in sets a cookie called singit_session that lasts 12 hours. We keep only a hash of the session token, with its expiry.
  • If you came from a link with campaign tags (utm_source and similar) or from another website, the first such source is saved with your account when it is created, to see which channels bring users. It holds no more than the tag values or the referring site’s host name.
  • If you turn on notifications: the push address and encryption keys your browser gives us.
  • If you save an email address in the app so the assistant can send you things, or buy a gift card: that address, encrypted at rest.

Limits, agent keys and payments

  • The limits you set for SingIt’s own agent (daily cap, per-purchase cap, expiry), the address of your limiter contract on Base, and the state of each grant, revoke or pause.
  • An agent key we create for your account, stored encrypted on our server so the agent can spend inside the budget you approved.
  • Payment records: amount, network, time, the transaction hash, the address paid and a label of what was paid for (for example a model answer, a data lookup or a gift card). Purchases made in the web app show up in your purchase history. For a gift card or eSIM, the code is shown to you once and is not stored by us. Until you reveal it, we keep an encrypted access token for the order, and we clear it when the code has been shown.
  • Prepaid balance: top-ups, per-request charges (model, token counts, cost, time and which key was used) and refund requests.
  • Optional starter credit, if we offer it to you: you link a GitHub account, and we read its numeric id, user name, creation date and whether it has a public repository of its own. We keep the id, user name and creation date with the claim and its decisions. The GitHub access token is kept only in memory and is not stored.

API keys and the MCP server

  • API keys are shown once and stored only as hashes, together with the key’s name, first characters, limits, scopes, expiry, last-used time, revocation and daily spend.
  • For each billed request we record the model, token counts, cost and time. We do not record the prompt or the answer. The MCP server is stateless: one request, one answer, nothing kept between calls except usage counts.

Chats in the web app

  • Chat titles, your messages and the answers, stored on our server so you can return to them. The assistant also sees up to your last 20 messages in a chat to answer. Deleting a chat in the app deletes its title and messages. Usage figures for it (model, tokens, cost) stay with your payment records.
  • A few things the assistant does for you need details you type in: for example an email address to send something to yourself, a place or flight for a lookup, or a phone number for a call (a limited pilot; calls are not recorded, and we keep a hash of the number and its last four digits).

Pay-per-request API (ask.singitai.app)

  • A paid x402 request carries your wallet address and a signed payment. In the standard flow the service does not store your prompt, the answer, your address or your IP address. It keeps hourly counters of calls, 402 responses and volume per route and model for 30 days, running totals, and a count of distinct paying wallets that uses a salted, truncated one-way hash. The salt is random and kept on our server.
  • If enabled, one anonymous line per answer is appended to a file for pricing research: the UTC hour, route, model, network, token counts and cost. It contains no question, answer, address, transaction or IP address.
  • On the Solana lane used by the web app, an answer is prepared first and handed over once payment is verified. The answer text is therefore held briefly in a private database on our server alongside a hash of the request, the owner and agent addresses, and the payment receipt.
  • Free answers (the free/ models) are limited per visitor network per day. The limit counts your network (the IPv4 /24 or IPv6 /64 of your address) in memory only; the address is not stored.

Telegram bot and approval channels

  • If you use the SingIt Telegram bot: your Telegram user id and user name, the managed wallet we create for you (address, with the private key encrypted at rest), spending limits and their usage, purchase history, an email address if you give one for Bitrefill, and the messages and commands you send. For gift cards and eSIMs bought this way, we keep an encrypted access token for the order until you reveal the code, which is shown once and not stored by us.
  • If you link a WhatsApp or iMessage approval channel: your phone number or contact identifier, the pairing code and your approve or reject decisions.
  • Free-form messages to the bot (not the fixed commands) can be sent to an AI model provider to produce a reply. The bot’s own history and logs on our server may contain messages you sent to it.

Technical data

  • Our servers see your IP address and browser details while handling a request. We use the IP address for rate limits and abuse prevention, in memory. We do not write it to our databases. Cloudflare, which sits in front of our servers, processes it as well (see below).
  • Service logs record errors and availability events, not request bodies. They can contain account identifiers, which include your wallet address.

Why we use it, and the legal basis

  • To provide what you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), contract): signing you in, running your limits and agent, answering questions, searching, buying, keeping your chats and purchase history, and sending notifications you switched on.
  • To keep the service safe and working (Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interests): rate limits, abuse and fraud prevention, security, fixing errors, checking that payments settled, and measuring how the product is used with cookieless analytics. We have weighed these against your rights and keep what we collect to what is needed for them.
  • To meet legal duties (Art. 6(1)(c)): accounting records, and responding to lawful requests from authorities.
  • With your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): browser notifications and linking an optional channel such as GitHub, WhatsApp or iMessage. You can withdraw it at any time, for example by turning notifications off or unlinking the channel, without affecting what was done before.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising. We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects on automated grounds. The agent buys only inside the limits you set and approve from your wallet.

Who else receives data

We pass on only what a request needs. These are the providers our code and configuration use today.

  • Model and data providers. Your prompt goes to the model that answers it. For the API, the app chat and the MCP server, models are mostly reached through Surplus Intelligence (api.surplusintelligence.ai), a marketplace that routes the request to a seller, and private chat can go through Venice AI. In the web app, your message is first read by TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai) to pick the intent, and OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) writes conversational replies and extracts search details. The Telegram bot uses OpenRouter. Each provider handles the content under its own policy.
  • Search and data sellers. Web search and page reading go to Exa (api.exa.ai). Some lookups in the app buy data from x402 sellers such as Otto AI (weather, rates, markets, news), StableTravel (flight status and prices), StableEmail (emails you ask the assistant to send to yourself) and StablePhone with Bland AI (calls). Your query, a place, a flight number or the text to send goes to them, and the payment shows your agent’s address.
  • Merchants. Gift cards, eSIMs and top-ups are bought from Bitrefill (api.bitrefill.com), which receives the order and the email address you gave for delivery.
  • Payment and blockchain infrastructure. The Coinbase Developer Platform x402 facilitator verifies and settles payments and sees the signed payment, the paying address and the amount. Base and Solana RPC nodes, Chainlink price feeds on Base and block explorers are read to check balances, limits and transactions. Reown / WalletConnect runs the wallet connection and the email and social wallets in the app, and a card on-ramp offered through it is run by the on-ramp provider, not by us.
  • GitHub, only if you use the starter credit link.
  • Messaging platforms. Telegram carries the bot’s messages. Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API) and the iMessage provider carry approval messages if you link those.
  • Hosting and delivery. Cloudflare serves the website, and our API and service endpoints are published through a Cloudflare tunnel, so Cloudflare handles the traffic and your IP address. Our services run on a single virtual server (VPS) where the databases live. Pages in the app load some libraries and fonts from jsDelivr and Google Fonts, which see your IP address when they do. Browser push messages travel through the push service of your browser (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft), end-to-end encrypted.
  • Email. Messages you send to contact@singitai.app pass through our email provider and any forwarding service we use for that address.
  • Authorities and advisers, when the law requires, to protect users or the service, or in a reorganisation, with suitable safeguards.

This list can change as models, sellers and merchants change. We update it when they do.

Public blockchains

Payments, limiter contracts and approvals are transactions on Base and Solana. Wallet addresses, amounts, times and transaction hashes are public to anyone and permanent. We cannot delete or change them, and a deletion request to us does not reach them. An address that pays SingIt can be linked to its other on-chain activity by anyone. Use a wallet you are comfortable with that in mind.

SingIt can show you a spending report for your own wallet (totals by category, the limit you set, what is left today). It is available only to the owner of the wallet: in the app after you sign in, or with an API key of the same account. It never includes chat content.

How long we keep it

We have no automatic deletion for most account and payment records. We keep each kind as long as it is needed for the purpose above, and then delete or anonymise it. What the code does today:

  • Sessions end after 12 hours. Sign-in challenges and link codes are cleaned up after they expire.
  • Chats stay until you delete them. Hashed API keys stay until you revoke them, and their records, with the usage and payment records, are kept for accounting, security and dispute handling.
  • Wallet addresses, limits, agent keys and payment records stay while your account exists and as long afterwards as we need them for accounting and legal reasons.
  • Pay-per-request counters: hourly figures for 30 days, running totals without addresses, and anonymous lines for pricing research with no fixed expiry.
  • Product analytics events and Umami data have no automatic expiry at present.
  • Service logs rotate with the server’s own limits, with no fixed period.
  • Telegram lane: daily spend records are cut after 30 days; wallets, purchase history, pairings and the bot’s history stay until deleted at your request.

When you ask us to delete data, we delete what we do not need to keep (see Your rights).

Analytics, cookies and local storage

  • Umami, self-hosted on our own server at stats.singitai.app, without cookies. It records page views and events with the page address, referrer, browser, operating system, device and screen size, language, and a country or region worked out from your IP address. We understand it does not store the IP address itself; its data stays on our server and we do not send it to anyone else.
  • Our server also records product events (such as sign-in, limits set, a purchase completed, an MCP tool call) in our own database. An account is identified there only by a salted one-way hash. Wallet addresses, email addresses, transaction hashes, prompts and chat content are never put into these events. The sign-in event passes your IP address and browser string to Umami so it can count the visit.
  • Cookies: only singit_session (your sign-in, 12 hours, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict), and a short-lived singit_starter_oauth cookie while you link GitHub. Both are strictly necessary. We set no advertising cookies.
  • Local storage in your browser: your session’s CSRF token and account label, the first campaign source, a few app preferences, and the wallet connection that Reown stores so a reload can reconnect. Clear it by signing out or clearing site data.

Security

Session tokens and API keys are stored only as hashes. Agent keys, managed wallet keys and saved email addresses are encrypted at rest, and database files are private to the service account. Traffic is encrypted in transit, and our services are published through a Cloudflare tunnel rather than on open ports. The limits you approve are enforced on chain on Base, and by our server on Solana. No system is perfectly secure. Report a security problem privately to contact@singitai.app.

Transfers outside the EU

Several providers listed above, including model providers, Cloudflare and Coinbase, process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses that those providers offer. Blockchains are global by design.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to your data and a copy, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and portability, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Where processing rests on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

  • In the app: delete chats, revoke API keys and allowances, turn notifications off and unlink channels. You can also revoke a token approval on chain without us, for example on revoke.cash.
  • By email: write to contact@singitai.app from the address you want us to act on, with the wallet address of your account (or your Telegram user name). To protect you, we may ask you to sign a message with that wallet. See also data deletion instructions.
  • We answer within one month. We can keep what the law or fraud prevention requires, and we cannot delete what is on a public blockchain.
  • Complaints: you have the right to complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (ÚOOU), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz, or to the authority in the EU country where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Giving us your wallet address and the data a request needs is necessary to use the service. Without it, we cannot run your account.

Age

SingIt is for people aged 18 and over, as set out in the Terms. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think someone younger has used SingIt, tell us and we will delete the data we can.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the service, our providers or the law change. The effective date changes when we do, and for material changes we will say so on the website.
