AI agent spending limit

Give your AI agent a spending limit, not your wallet

Set a daily limit and a per-purchase limit once. Your agent spends USDC inside them without asking again, and one signature takes it all back.

The problem.

  • An agent with a funded hot wallet or a stored API key is a blank check. A leaked key is lost money.

  • Limits that live in the agent's own software are only as strong as that software, and as the prompt it was given.

  • Spend alerts don't help at 3am. By the time you read one, the money is gone.

How it works.

  1. Sign inwith the wallet you already use, or with email, Google or Apple. Signing in is a message signature and moves nothing.
  2. Set your limits:daily, per purchase, and how many days they last.
  3. Approve once.One approval from your wallet. From then on the agent buys inside the limits.
  4. Revoke any timewith one more signature, in SingIt or without us on revoke.cash.

Base vs Solana

Aspect Base Solana
What you approve A USDC allowance to your own limiter contract, deployed for you (SingIt pays its gas) The agent as delegate on your USDC account, for a total you choose
Who enforces the limits The contract, on chain: daily limit, per-purchase limit and expiry are fixed in it The chain caps the approved total; SingIt enforces the daily and per-purchase limits before every payment

The one function that moves money

On Base, the limiter is a small contract with no admin, no proxy and no setter. It holds no tokens: each purchase goes from your wallet to the seller in one transferFrom.

function spend(address payee, uint256 amount, bytes32 ref) external {
    if (msg.sender != agent) revert NotAgent();
    if (paused) revert IsPaused();
    if (block.timestamp >= expiry) revert Expired();
    if (amount > perPurchaseCap) revert PerPurchaseCapExceeded();
    // ...
    uint256 today = block.timestamp / 1 days;
    uint256 spent = today == day ? spentToday : 0;
    if (spent + amount > dailyCap) revert DailyCapExceeded();
    // ...
    if (!token.transferFrom(owner, payee, amount)) revert TransferFailed();
}

A watcher that reads the chain

A watcher reads the chain, not SingIt's own records. On Base, a spend to anyone but your own agent, or an unusual burst of spends, pauses the limiter and alerts you. The guardian key can pause and nothing else: it can't move funds or raise limits.

What the budget pays for.

  • Gift cards, eSIMs and mobile top-ups through Bitrefill (details).

  • Live answers (weather, exchange rates, flight status, places), each one small paid x402 request to a checked seller at a capped price.

  • A chat that bills by the answer, and Venice for private chat, paid from the same limits.

FAQ.

Can SingIt or the agent take more than my limits?

On Base, no: the caps and expiry are fixed in the contract and hold even if SingIt's server or the agent is compromised. On Solana, the chain caps the approved total, and SingIt enforces the daily and per-purchase limits.

How do I change a limit?

Nothing in the Base limiter can change after deployment. You set new limits, SingIt deploys a new limiter, and you approve it. The old limiter is superseded, and its approval can be set to zero.

How do I stop everything?

Revoke with one signature in SingIt, or on revoke.cash without us. An emergency stop pauses a Base limiter for good. Resuming means a new limiter and a new approval.

When does the daily limit reset?

On Base, at UTC midnight (the contract counts calendar days, not a rolling 24 hours).

Does setting up cost gas?

On Base, SingIt deploys your limiter and pays its gas. The approval is a normal USDC approve from your wallet.
