Legal

Data deletion

You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with your SingIt account.

How to submit a request

  1. Email contact@singitai.app.
  2. Use the subject line “SingIt data deletion request”.
  3. Include the wallet address of your SingIt account (or your Telegram user name if you used the Telegram bot), so we can find the records.
  4. We may ask you to sign a message with that wallet, or to write from a verified address, to confirm the account is yours.

You can also delete chats, revoke API keys and allowances and turn off notifications yourself in the app. We answer requests within one month.

Privacy contact
contact@singitai.app

We may retain records required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes. Information permanently recorded on a public blockchain cannot be deleted or modified by SingIt.
