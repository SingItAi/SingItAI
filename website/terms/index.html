Some features do hold funds or keys for you: a prepaid API-key balance, if you top one up; an agent key that SingIt stores encrypted on its server so that SingIt’s own agent can spend inside the budget you approved; and a small working balance that your agent keeps to pay micro-payments (it is refilled to about 0.20 USDC by default on Base, and a larger purchase moves its full amount to the agent first).   • You are responsible for your wallet, its keys and everything signed with them. We never ask for your seed phrase or private key; do not send them to us.
  • API keys act for your account inside the daily limit, per-request limit and expiry set when the key is created. Keep keys secret and revoke any key you think has leaked. Anything done with a key counts as done by you.
  • When you give SingIt’s own agent a budget, you set a per-purchase cap, a daily cap and an expiry, and approve it from your wallet. On Base the limiter contract deployed for you enforces these limits on chain on each transfer from your wallet to your agent; what your agent already holds can then be spent without another contract check. On Solana the chain enforces only the total you approve as an SPL delegate; the daily and per-purchase limits and the expiry are enforced by SingIt’s server. You can revoke the approval at any time in SingIt or without SingIt, for example on revoke.cash. Details: agent spending limits.

Prices and payments

  • Every paid x402 request is quoted before you pay: a request without payment returns HTTP 402 with the price, network, asset and recipient. Calls made with an API key are charged to its prepaid balance at the listed price, and purchases such as gift cards are shown with their price for you to confirm before they are bought. Prices are also listed on Models & prices and Pricing. They can change; the quote in the 402 you sign is the price that applies to that call. Prices can include a service margin and a settlement fee.
  • Some routes authorize a maximum and charge only what the request used (“upto”), never more than the maximum you signed. Other routes charge a flat amount (“exact”), with no metering and no refund of the difference.
  • If a request fails on our side before it is answered, we try not to charge you. For the standard x402 “upto” flow on Base nothing is settled, and on Solana the escrowed amount is refunded in full. This is not guaranteed for every route or failure, for example when a payment cannot be resolved automatically; write to us and we will look at it.
  • Payments settle on public blockchains and cannot be reversed. We do not refund a payment that has settled on chain for a request that was served, including an answer that turns out to be wrong or unsatisfactory. If you think you were charged by mistake, write to us and we will look at it, but we are not obliged to refund.
  • Some accounts can top up a prepaid USDC balance for API keys. You can ask for an unspent balance to be returned from the app; the balance is then frozen and we send it back to your wallet by hand, so it is not instant and we give no fixed time. Promotional or starter credit, if we offer any, has no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.
  • Network fees charged by the blockchain, and any fees charged by your wallet, are not ours and are your cost.
